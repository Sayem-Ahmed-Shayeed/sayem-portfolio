CamelliaOfSylhet is an unlabelled, in-the-wild tea-leaf image dataset collected from tea gardens in Sylhet, Bangladesh under natural field conditions. It contains 1,637 original field photographs, including 1,504 HEIC images and 133 JPEG images, and supports future agricultural computer-vision research in self-supervised learning, domain-specific pretraining, leaf detection and segmentation.
